03 · The statistical engine

Every score has an audit trail.

The model is deterministic JavaScript, not a predictive black box. It rebuilds innings in sequence, measures difficulty, compares like with like, then applies safeguards against small samples.

01

Rebuild

Score, wickets and legal balls are known before each delivery.

02

Benchmark

Expected outcomes come from comparable match states, excluding the rated player.

03

Residualise

Runs, wickets and dismissals become value above or below fair expectation.

04

Trust carefully

Effective sample size, shrinkage and match consistency protect the ranking.

The safeguards

Why a hot cameo cannot win the model

  • 70 effective-ball shrinkageThin evidence is pulled toward caution.
